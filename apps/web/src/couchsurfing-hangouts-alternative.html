A Couchsurfing Hangouts alternative for meeting travelers

Hangouts shows who's nearby and up to meet right now. If that's the part of Couchsurfing you used, Tablamigo does the same job with plans, and it's free to join them.

We make Tablamigo, so keep that in mind. Couchsurfing details come from its App Store and Google Play listings and the Couchsurfing blog, checked on September 28, 2026.

The short answer

  • Keep Couchsurfing if you want to stay with locals. Tablamigo doesn't do stays at all.
  • Try Tablamigo if you only want company: someone to eat with tonight or share a day trip. Joining plans is free, while Couchsurfing's blog says Hangouts and Events need a paid membership.

Is Couchsurfing still free?

Not for meeting people. A Couchsurfing blog post updated in September 2026 says "a paid Membership unlocks free accommodations, as well as Events, Communities, Hangouts, and Couch Crashes." Hosts still don't charge for the stay. On the US App Store, Couchsurfing's in-app purchases range from $1.99 to $29.99, including monthly, quarterly and annual contributions and an annual verification.

Where Couchsurfing is better

  • Stays. Nothing on Tablamigo replaces a local host.
  • References. Couchsurfing has been running since 2003, and members' written references build up over years.
  • Locals. Hosts and many event organizers on Couchsurfing live in the city. Tablamigo is built for travelers and digital nomads.
  • Size. Its Google Play listing shows more than 5 million downloads. Tablamigo is new.

Looking for free stays without Couchsurfing? Couchsurfing's own blog lists the nonprofit hospitality networks Trustroots and BeWelcome. Couchers.org is another: it describes itself as free, community-led and non-profit, run by volunteers as a project of Couchers, Inc., a US 501(c)(3) non-profit, and it has hosting and events (couchers.org, checked October 11, 2026).

Questions

What is the best alternative to Couchsurfing Hangouts?

For meeting travelers nearby without a membership, try a plan-based app like Tablamigo or Nomadtable, where you join an activity someone posted. Hostelworld's chats are another option if you stay in hostels.

Do I need a paid membership for Couchsurfing Hangouts?

Yes. Couchsurfing's blog says a paid membership unlocks Hangouts, Events, Communities and stays with hosts.

Does anyone still use Couchsurfing?

Yes. Its Google Play listing shows more than 5 million downloads and the app was updated in September 2026. How active it is varies by city.

Does Couchsurfing work in India?

Yes, the app is on Google Play and the App Store in India, and Google Play India listed its in-app purchases at ₹290 to ₹3,400 per item on October 11, 2026. Hangouts and Events still need a paid membership, and how many hosts are active varies a lot by town. For meeting other travelers in India without one, see our comparison of travel buddy apps in India and where solo travelers meet in India.

Can I find a place to stay on Tablamigo?

No. Tablamigo is only for meeting people for activities like meals, drinks, day trips and coworking. Please don't use plans to ask for a place to stay.
