Tablamigo: about the solo-travel app

How Tablamigo works

  1. Open the map. You see plans that travelers near you have posted, listed by start time with plans from Plus members first, with who's going and roughly how far away it is.
  2. Join, or post your own. Joining puts you in the plan's group chat. Posting takes a minute: pick what, where and when, and people nearby can join.
  3. Agree the details. Use the chat, run a poll to pick a time, and share your live location for 15 minutes, an hour or 8 hours if it helps people find you.
  4. Plan ahead. Add a trip to see who'll be in the same city on your dates, and join that city's chat before you land.

There's also a list of travelers nearby with an age filter, direct messages, AI plan ideas for your city, and women-only plans. The full feature list is in the fact sheet.

What we care about

  • Plans first, profiles second. You decide what to join based on what's happening, not on photos.
  • Not a dating app. Plans are for food, drinks, sightseeing, sport and coworking, and turning a plan into something else can be reported.
  • Privacy by default. Other travelers see a distance rounded to the nearest 0.5 km, not where you are, and plans show only the meeting place the host chose. You can choose to share your live location in a chat.
  • Women-only plans. Women can host plans that only other women can see and join.
  • Moderation you can see. Group and city chat messages are checked by AI moderation before they post, you can report with a choice of six reasons or block in one tap, and every report goes to our team. More on our safety page.

What Tablamigo isn't

  • It isn't a tour company. We don't organise, host or vet meetups; travelers post and join plans themselves.
  • It doesn't book travel, hostels or tickets.
  • It isn't big yet. Tablamigo is new, and we don't publish user numbers, ratings or awards. In many cities you'll be posting the first plan, and we'd rather tell you that than pretend otherwise.
