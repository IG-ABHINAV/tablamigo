Tablamigo safety tools and tips

Most people on Tablamigo are just travelers looking for company. These habits keep it that way.

What Tablamigo does

  • 18+ only. Age is checked at sign-up.
  • No exact locations. Other people only see a distance rounded to 0.5 km, not where you are, unless you choose to share live location in a chat.
  • Women-only plans. Women can host plans that only women see and join.
  • Report and block in one tap. Blocking hides you from each other everywhere in the app. The other person isn't told.
  • Selfie verification. Take a selfie copying a pose we show you. Our own servers compare it with your profile photo, and a match gives you a ✅ next to your name within seconds. It's optional, so check for the badge before you meet someone.
  • Moderated group chats. Messages in plan and city chats are checked by AI moderation before they post, and messages flagged for harassment, hate or sexual content are blocked. If the check can't be completed in time, the message still posts, so report anything that gets through. Direct messages aren't sent for AI moderation.
  • Live location you control. Share it with a plan chat or a direct message for 15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours. Updates stop when the time ends or when you tap Stop, and after that the chat shows only that you shared your location, not where you were. People you have blocked never receive it. It's never offered in city chats.
  • A human reviews reports and bans accounts that break our rules. You can report a person, a plan or a message, with a choice of six reasons.
  • Snooze. Take a break and disappear from the map and the travelers list without deleting anything.

Before you go

  • Meet in busy, public places: a restaurant, bar, market or popular sight. Not a private home or hotel room.
  • Keep the conversation in the Tablamigo chat until you're comfortable, so you can report it if something goes wrong.
  • Tell a friend where you're going and who with.
  • Look at the person's profile. Blank profiles and brand-new accounts deserve more caution.

When you're there

  • Get yourself there and back. Don't depend on someone you just met for a ride.
  • Watch your drink and know your limits.
  • Never send money to someone you met on Tablamigo.
  • Trust your gut. You can leave at any time, and you don't owe anyone an explanation.

Questions

Can other people see my exact location on Tablamigo?

Not unless you choose to share it live. Other travelers only see a distance rounded to the nearest 0.5 km, and plans show only the meeting place the host chose. Live location is shared only when you choose to, in a plan chat or direct message, and stops after 15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours or when you tap Stop. After that the chat shows only that you shared your location, not where you were.

How does Tablamigo verify people?

Verification is optional. You take a selfie copying a pose we show you, and our own servers check it against your profile photo. If it matches, you get a verified badge within seconds. The badge shows that the selfie matched your profile photo, nothing more. No outside face-recognition service is used, and the selfie is stored with our cloud storage provider like your other photos.

What happens when I report someone?

The report goes to our team with the reason you picked. Accounts that break our rules are banned. The person you report isn't told who reported them.

What happens when I block someone?

You disappear from each other everywhere in the app: plans, chats, the map and the travelers list. The other person isn't told.

Is Tablamigo safe for women traveling alone?

Women can host women-only plans that only other women can see and join, and every safety tool on this page applies. Gender is what each person picks on their profile and we don't verify it, so report anyone who misuses women-only plans. See our solo female travel guide, and for India, solo female travel in India.

Is it safe to travel with people I met on Tablamigo?

Meet over a plan in a public place first, and don't book a trip together until you've spent some time with them. Keep your own bookings and money, share your itinerary with someone at home, and never send money in advance. In India, our page on solo trips with strangers has a checklist for group trips too.

In danger? Call local emergency services first (112 works across Europe, in India and in many other countries). Then report the account in the app. Tablamigo can't call emergency services or contact anyone for you.
