What is Tablamigo?

Tablamigo is a solo-travel app for meeting people nearby. Solo travelers and digital nomads post plans such as dinner, drinks, day trips or coworking on a map, and others nearby join with one tap and talk in the plan's group chat. It launched on 29 September 2026, runs on Android (Tablamigo on Google Play) and runs in any web browser, including Safari on iPhone and Mac, and is made by Hookstep in Mumbai, India. Questions

Is it Tablamigo or Table Amigo?

It is Tablamigo, one word, and you may also see it written Tabl Amigo or Table Amigo. They all mean the same solo-travel app, so any of those spellings will find the same Tablamigo app on Google Play and the same website.

Is Tablamigo a dating app?

No. Tablamigo is for finding company for real activities: food, drinks, sightseeing, sport, coworking. Plans are usually groups, and anything that turns a plan into something else can be reported.

Can people see where I am?

Not exactly. Other travelers only see a distance rounded to the nearest half kilometer, and plans only show the meeting place the host chose. The one exception is live location, which you can choose to share in a plan chat or direct message for 15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours.

Who can use Tablamigo?

Adults aged 18 and over. We check age at sign-up and remove accounts that break this rule.

Is Tablamigo on iPhone?

Not as an app yet. On iPhone, open the Tablamigo web app in Safari; add it to your Home Screen to get notifications.

How do I cancel Plus?

If you bought Plus in the Android app from Google Play, open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Tablamigo and cancel. If you paid on the website or in the web app (through Razorpay), cancel under Manage subscription. Either way you keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

Where does Tablamigo work?

Anywhere. The map shows plans near wherever you are, and it's busiest where solo travelers already gather. Tablamigo is new, so if there's nothing near you yet, post a plan and the next traveler nearby will see it. Browse our solo travel city guides for ideas.
