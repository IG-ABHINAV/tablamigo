Tablamigo partner programme

Creators, travel writers and community hosts: share your own Tablamigo link and earn 30% of what the people you bring pay us, for as long as you are a partner.

Become a partner

You join inside the Tablamigo web app or Android app, signed in to your account (Profile › Partner programme). It takes one step: read the terms below, accept them, and your link is ready.

How it works

  1. Join in one step. Accept the partner terms in the app and you get your own code and link, like https://tablamigo.hookstep.in/?ref=YOURCODE.
  2. Share it. Anyone who opens your link and creates an account, on the web or by installing the Android app from it, counts as yours. People can also type your code in their first days on Tablamigo.
  3. Earn 30%. When they buy Tablamigo Plus or a Trip Pass, 30% of what they pay us (after Google Play's or the payment provider's fee) is yours, on every payment, while you are a partner.
  4. Get paid monthly. By bank transfer or UPI, once you are owed ₹1,000 or $12. Add your payout details in the app; they are stored encrypted and shown to you masked.

Your partner page in the app shows your link with a copy button, how many people signed up, how many pay, and what you have earned, are owed and were paid, in each currency.

Partner terms

Dated 4 November 2026. These are the terms you accept in the app. They sit alongside the Tablamigo terms of service.

  1. You earn 30% of what the people you bring to Tablamigo pay us (after Google Play's or the payment provider's fee), for as long as you are a partner.
  2. People count as yours when they create their account from your link, install the Android app from it, or enter your code in their first days on Tablamigo. One partner per person: the first one counts.
  3. You never earn on your own purchases or your own account, and two partners cannot be each other's referrals.
  4. Refunded or charged-back payments earn nothing; if one was already paid out, it comes off your next payout.
  5. We pay monthly by bank transfer or UPI once you are owed ₹1,000 or $12. Other currencies are paid in INR at the rate on the payout day.
  6. No paid promotion: no ads, sponsored posts or search ads that use the Tablamigo name. No spam, fake reviews or misleading claims, and no cashback or coupon sites.
  7. We can pause or end a partnership that breaks these terms. Commission stops for payments after that; what you already earned fairly is still paid.

Questions

Who can become a partner?

Anyone with a Tablamigo account. There is no application form and no follower minimum: open Profile › Partner programme in the app, accept the terms, and your link is ready.

How long do I earn from someone I brought?

For as long as you are a partner: every payment they make, including renewals, earns you 30%. If your partnership is paused or ended, payments after that earn nothing.

Do the people I bring get a discount?

No. Your link records that they came from you; they pay the normal price, and nothing changes for them.

Can I run ads with my link?

No. There is no paid promotion: share it with your own audience, in your own posts, videos, newsletters and groups.

Are link clicks counted?

Not yet. Your partner page counts sign-ups, paying customers and commission. Customers are never named to you.

How do I get paid?

Monthly, by bank transfer or UPI to an Indian account, once you are owed ₹1,000 or $12. Questions about a payout: support@hookstep.in.
