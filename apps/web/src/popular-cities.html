Popular cities on Tablamigo

Where travelers on Tablamigo are heading next, ranked by upcoming trips, then by plans in the coming week.

How this list is counted

  • Only real travelers count. The Tablamigo team's account, demo and test accounts, store review accounts, deleted and banned accounts, and anyone who has hidden their profile are left out.
  • An upcoming trip is a trip that hasn't ended yet. Each traveler counts once per city, however many trips they added.
  • Plans this week are open plans starting in the next 7 days, hosted by real travelers.
  • A city is listed only once 10 or more travelers have upcoming trips there, so small numbers about a few people are never published.
