A solo female travel app with women-only plans

On Tablamigo, a woman can post a plan that only other women can see and join: dinner, a sunrise hike, a night market. Here's exactly how that works, the other safety tools, and their limits.

In short: women-only plans are hidden from the map and lists for everyone whose profile doesn't say they're a woman, and only women can host or join them. Add selfie verification, AI moderation of group chats, rounded distances and one-tap report and block, and you have more control over who you meet. It doesn't replace meeting in public and trusting your gut.

How women-only plans work

  1. Post a plan and switch on "Women only". Only accounts whose profile gender is "woman" can do this.
  2. It's hidden from everyone else. Men and people who chose another gender don't see the plan on the map or in lists.
  3. Only women can join. If someone else gets a direct link, the app still won't let them join.
  4. You get a group chat with everyone who joins, to agree the time and place.

One honest limit: gender is what each person picks on their profile. We don't check ID, and the verified badge doesn't change that: it shows that a selfie matched the person's profile photo, not their gender. Reports of anyone misusing women-only plans go straight to our team.

Other safety tools

  • Selfie verification. Take a selfie copying a pose we show you. It's compared with your profile photo automatically, and if it matches you get a ✅ next to your name within seconds. Check for the badge before you meet someone.
  • AI moderation of group chats. Messages in plan chats and city chats are checked before they post. Harassment, hate and sexual content get blocked. If the check can't be completed in time, the message still posts. Direct messages aren't sent for AI moderation, so report anything that crosses a line.
  • No exact location. Others only see a distance rounded to 0.5 km, unless you choose to share live location in a chat. A plan shows the meeting place the host chose, not where anyone is.
  • Report and block. Report with one of six reasons, including harassment, "looks under 18" and fake profile. Blocking hides you from each other everywhere in the app, and they aren't told.
  • Live location, on your terms. Share it with a plan's chat for 15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours so your group can find you. It stops on its own, and after that the chat shows only that you shared your location, not where you were.
  • Snooze. Disappear from the map for a day, a week or until you turn it off, without deleting anything.
  • 18+ only, checked at sign-up.

Safety tips for meeting people as a solo woman

  • Pick group plans in busy places for a first meetup: a food market, a walking tour, a popular bar.
  • Keep the chat in the app until you've met. If something goes wrong, you can report the message itself.
  • Tell someone at home where you're going and who with. Sharing live location with a friend in the chat works too.
  • Get yourself there and back. Book your own ride home before the evening starts.
  • Watch your drink, and never send money to someone you met in an app.
  • Leave whenever you want. You don't owe anyone an explanation.

Questions

Is there a women-only travel app?

Yes. NomadHer and Travel Ladies are for women only. Tablamigo is open to everyone but lets women post plans that only other women can see and join.

How can I find a female travel companion?

Post a women-only plan on Tablamigo for the day trip or dinner you want company for, and women nearby can join. For a companion for a whole trip, Travel Ladies lets you filter travel buddies by destination and dates.

Can men see women-only plans on Tablamigo?

Not on the map or in any list. Women-only plans are hidden from anyone whose profile gender isn't "woman", and the app won't let them join, even with a direct link.

Does Tablamigo verify gender?

No. Gender is what each person chooses on their profile, and we don't check ID. Selfie verification confirms that someone looks like their photos. Report anyone misusing women-only plans and our team will act on it.

Is it safe to meet people from a travel app as a woman?

Most people are just travelers looking for company, but take the same care you would anywhere: meet in busy public places, pick group plans at first, keep chats in the app, tell someone where you're going and get yourself home. Look at profiles first (the selfie-verified badge only shows that a selfie matched the profile photo) and report anything that feels off.
