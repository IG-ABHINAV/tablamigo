A Timeleft alternative for dinner with strangers, any night

Timeleft matches you with 4 to 6 strangers for dinner every Wednesday. Tablamigo is for the other six nights: see who near you wants to eat tonight, or post "street food at 7" and let people join.

We make Tablamigo, so keep that in mind. Timeleft details come from its App Store and Google Play listings and timeleft.com, checked on September 28, 2026.

The short answer

  • Choose Timeleft if you want someone else to do everything: it matches you by a personality quiz, picks the restaurant and tells you where to go on the day. It works best as a weekly habit in the city you're living in.
  • Try Tablamigo if you're traveling and want company tonight. You see the plan, the place and who's going before you join, and joining is free.

Where Timeleft is better

  • No effort. You don't plan anything or talk to anyone before the dinner.
  • Matching. Tables are built from a personality quiz. Tablamigo doesn't match you with anyone; you choose.
  • Scale. Its listing says it runs in 52 countries, and it has 8,557 App Store ratings. Tablamigo is new, so in many cities you'll be the one posting the first dinner.
  • Repeat meetups. Rebooking each week with the same people is built in.

Why travelers use a plan app instead

Say you land in Lisbon on a Thursday and leave on Monday. A Wednesday dinner is no help. On Tablamigo you can post "Seafood in Cais do Sodré, 8pm" as soon as you've dropped your bags, and anyone nearby can join and sort out the details in the plan's group chat. Timeleft in India: cities, price and alternatives

Checked on October 6, 2026 against timeleft.com and Timeleft's listings on the Indian App Store and on Google Play as shown in India.

Cities. Timeleft's locations page lists four Indian cities: Bangalore, Hyderabad, Mumbai and New Delhi. No other Indian city is listed, so going by that page, Pune, Chennai, Goa and Kolkata aren't covered. The app is on both the Indian App Store and Google Play. We haven't booked a Timeleft dinner in India, so we can't say how often tables run in each city or how full they are.

Price. We found no rupee price on timeleft.com. Its blog puts a membership at around $20 a month in the US and says prices vary by region and show in the app. The Indian App Store lists three in-app purchases, at ₹1,099, ₹2,599 and ₹3,500, and the Indian Google Play listing shows in-app purchases from ₹1,850 to ₹6,200 per item. Neither listing says which purchase covers how many months, so check the price in the app before you pay. Food and drinks at the restaurant are on top.

Alternatives in India. If your city isn't on that list, or you want company on a night other than Wednesday, Tablamigo works in India on Android and in any browser, and it's free to use. It's new, so in many cities you'll be the one posting the first plan. See Tablamigo in India and our solo travel in India guide. If you want a matched table in Bangalore, Hyderabad, Mumbai or New Delhi, that's what Timeleft does and Tablamigo doesn't.

Questions

Is Timeleft available in India?

Yes, in four cities: Bangalore, Hyderabad, Mumbai and New Delhi, per timeleft.com's locations page (October 6, 2026). The app is on the Indian App Store and Google Play, and its in-app purchases are priced from ₹1,099 on the App Store and ₹1,850 on Google Play.

What is a good Timeleft alternative?

If you're traveling and want dinner company on a night that suits you, try a plan-based app like Tablamigo or Nomadtable. If you live somewhere and want interest groups, Meetup is free to join.

How much does Timeleft cost?

You pay for your booking in the Timeleft app, and the US App Store lists its in-app purchases at $19.99 to $62.99 (September 2026). Timeleft's website says you also pay for your own food and drinks at the venue.

Is there a free Timeleft alternative?

Tablamigo is free to join and post plans, including dinners. You pay only for your own meal. Plus, the optional subscription, adds more plans a day and trip features: {{plus_month}} a month or {{plus_year}} a year on the web, or a Google Play subscription in the Android app.

Is Timeleft for travelers or locals?

Timeleft says anyone 18+ looking to form new friendships can join. Its fixed weekly nights suit people who live in a city more than people passing through.

Is Tablamigo a dating app?

No. Tablamigo is for company on real activities: meals, drinks, sightseeing, sport and coworking. Plans are usually groups, and anything that turns a plan into something else can be reported.
