What a travel buddy app should do

Most solo travelers aren't looking for a pen pal, or even a travel partner for the whole trip. They want someone to share a taxi to the ruins with, or a table for street food at 7. A good travel buddy app gets you from stranger to plan quickly, and keeps you safe while you do it.

That's the idea behind Tablamigo: plans first, profiles second. You see what people are actually doing near you, and you decide what to join.

How to meet travelers with Tablamigo

  1. Open the map. See plans from travelers around you, with who's going and roughly how far away they are.
  2. Join or post a plan. Tap join and you're in the group chat. Or post "I want to…" and let people come to you.
  3. Agree the details in the chat. Use a poll to pick the time or place, then meet somewhere busy and public.
  4. Add your next trip. See who'll be in the same city on the same dates, so you land with plans already made.

Other good ways to meet travelers

An app works best alongside the classics. These are the places solo travelers have always met each other:

  • Hostel events. Family dinners, pub crawls and walking tours, even if you're not staying there.
  • Free walking tours. Two hours with a group is an easy start, and people often grab lunch after.
  • Group day trips and treks. Hikes, boat trips and tours are cheaper and more fun shared.
  • Coworking spaces and cafés, if you're working on the road.
  • Classes. Cooking, surfing, salsa or a language exchange.

Any of these can be a Tablamigo plan: post the walking tour or the cooking class and go with people instead of turning up alone. There's a longer list in our guide on how to meet people while traveling solo.

Meeting people safely

  • Meet in busy public places, not a private home or hotel room.
  • Keep the conversation in the app until you're comfortable, so you can report it if something goes wrong.
  • Tell a friend where you're going, and get yourself there and back.
  • Never send money to someone you met in the app.

Questions

What is a travel buddy app?

A travel buddy app helps you find other travelers to do things with, like sharing a meal, a day trip or a night out. Tablamigo does this with plans: travelers post what they want to do nearby, and others join with a tap and chat in a group.

How do I meet other travelers when I'm traveling alone?

Join something that's already happening. Open Tablamigo to see plans near you and join one, or post your own. Hostel events, free walking tours, group day trips and classes are other easy ways to meet people.

Can I find a travel partner for a whole trip on Tablamigo?

Tablamigo is built for company plan by plan, like a dinner, a day trip or a night out, not for matching you with one travel companion for a whole trip. Add your trip to see who'll be in the same city on your dates, join a few plans, and if you get on with someone you can keep talking in direct messages and plan the next stretch together.

How do I meet travelers near me?

Open Tablamigo and look at the map: it shows plans that travelers near you have posted, and you join one with a tap and land in its group chat. The travelers nearby list shows people who opened the app near you in the last 3 days, with an age filter. If nothing suits you, post your own plan with a place and a time.

Is Tablamigo a travel companion app?

It's for finding companions, not for planning the trip. Some apps sold as travel companions build itineraries or book flights and hotels; Tablamigo does neither. It shows you plans from travelers nearby and who'll be in your next city on your dates, so you have people to eat, explore and share a taxi with.

Can I find a travel buddy before my trip?

Yes. Add your trip to Tablamigo and see who'll be in the same city on the same dates. Free accounts see 3 people overlapping each trip; Plus shows everyone.

Most people are just travelers looking for company, but take the same care you would anywhere: meet in busy public places, keep chats in the app, tell a friend where you're going and trust your gut.
