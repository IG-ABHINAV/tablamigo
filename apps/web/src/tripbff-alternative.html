Looking for a TripBFF alternative?

TripBFF matches you with travelers by interests and destination. Tablamigo starts from plans: see what's happening near you, then join. Here's how they compare.

We make Tablamigo, so keep that in mind. TripBFF details come from its US App Store and Google Play listings, checked on September 28, 2026.

The short answer

  • Stay with TripBFF if you like browsing people and travel content before you meet, or you need an iPhone app. It's been on the App Store since June 2023 and has 3,902 ratings there (4.7 average) and 100,000+ Google Play downloads.
  • Try Tablamigo if you'd rather skip the matching and join a dinner or day trip that's already planned, with a group chat for each plan, women-only plans and AI moderation of group chats.

Where TripBFF is better

  • It has an iPhone app. Tablamigo only has a web app on iPhone for now.
  • It has a head start: over three years on the App Store while Tablamigo is launching now, and 3,902 App Store ratings where Tablamigo has none yet.
  • If you like reading other travelers' guides and tips inside the app, TripBFF has a feed for that. Tablamigo doesn't.

Questions

What is a good TripBFF alternative?

If you want to join plans rather than match with profiles, try Tablamigo or Nomadtable. Both show activities near you that you can join in one tap. Hostelworld's chats suit you if you stay in hostels.

Is there a TripBFF alternative for Android?

TripBFF itself is on Android. If you want something different, Tablamigo is on Google Play and works as a web app, and Nomadtable is on Google Play too.

How safe is TripBFF?

TripBFF's store listings don't describe verification or chat moderation, so we can't say. Whatever app you use, meet in busy public places, keep chats in the app, tell a friend where you're going and never send money to someone you've just met.

Nomadtable or TripBFF?

Nomadtable is built around joining activities near you; TripBFF is built around matching with travelers and a travel content feed. We compare Nomadtable in detail on our Nomadtable alternative page.
