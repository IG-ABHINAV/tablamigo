Tablamigo press kit and fact sheet

Everything you need to write about Tablamigo accurately. If something here is out of date or you need something else, email support@hookstep.in.

Boilerplate

Short (under 30 words): Tablamigo is a solo-travel app for meeting people nearby. Travelers post plans like dinner or a day trip on a map, and others join with one tap.

Spelling: Tablamigo, one word, capital T only. Not "Tab Amigo" or "TablAmigo".

Long (under 100 words): Tablamigo is a solo-travel app for meeting people nearby. Solo travelers and digital nomads post plans such as dinner, drinks, day trips or coworking on a map, and others nearby join with one tap and talk in the plan's group chat. It also shows who'll be in the same city on your trip dates, and women can host women-only plans. Distances are rounded to 0.5 km, selfie verification is optional, and group chats are moderated. Tablamigo launched on 29 September 2026 on Android and the web, and is made by Hookstep in Mumbai, India.

Features

  • A map of plans posted by travelers nearby (dinner, drinks, nightlife, day trips, sport, culture, coworking), listed by start time with plans from Plus members first
  • Join a plan with one tap and land in its group chat, or post your own plan in about a minute
  • A list of travelers nearby, free, with an age filter
  • Trips: add where you're going and see who'll be in the same city on the same dates
  • City chats for travelers arriving in a city, direct messages and chat polls
  • Live location sharing in plan chats and direct messages for 15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours, then it stops by itself
  • AI plan ideas for your city, ready to post in one tap
  • Women-only plans that only women can see, host or join
  • Snooze, to disappear from the map without deleting anything
  • Sign in with Google or with an email code

Safety

  • 18+ only, checked at sign-up
  • Other travelers see a distance rounded to 0.5 km, not your exact position, unless you choose to share your live location in a chat
  • Optional selfie verification: a selfie in a requested pose, checked automatically against the profile photo on Tablamigo's own servers
  • AI moderation of group and city chat messages before they post
  • Report with a choice of six reasons, or block in one tap; every report goes to the team
  • Not a dating app, under the terms of use
