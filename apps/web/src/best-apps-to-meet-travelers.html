The best apps to meet travelers in 2026

No single app wins everywhere. The right one depends on how long you're staying, where you sleep and whether you want other travelers or locals.

We make Tablamigo, and it's on this list, marked as ours. Nomadtable: best for joining plans with other travelers

Nomadtable is built around activities. Open it, see what travelers near you are doing (dinner, sightseeing, a night out), and join. You can also add your next destination and get put in a group chat with people there on the same dates. Its summer 2026 update added live location in chats, polls, trip timelines, verification badges and a travel map.

Good: more than 1 million Google Play downloads, more than 20,000 App Store ratings, and apps for iPhone and Android. Watch out for: the full list of nearby travelers and who viewed your profile need Plus, and public reviews mention people using it for dating, which its guidelines ban. Full details in our Nomadtable comparison.

Tablamigo (ours): best for a plan-first map on Android or the web

Tablamigo shows plans from travelers near you on a map, listed by start time with plans from Plus members first: street food at 7, a hike tomorrow, a coworking afternoon. Tap join and you're in that plan's group chat. There's also a free list of travelers nearby, trips that show who'll overlap with you, city chats, polls and live location sharing for 15 minutes, an hour or 8 hours.

Good: women-only plans that only women can see and join, AI moderation of group and city chats, distances rounded to 0.5 km, and a web app that runs in any browser, including on iPhone. Watch out for: it's brand new, so in many cities you'll be posting the first plan, and there's no iPhone app yet.

TripBFF: best for finding travelers heading where you are

TripBFF matches you with travelers based on your interests and destination wishlist, shows people nearby and on the same upcoming trips, and has group chats. It also has a feed of user-written travel guides and tips, and a "Travel Wrapped" recap.

Good: on the App Store since June 2023, with 100,000+ Google Play downloads. Watch out for: its App Store listing says a subscription can't be cancelled during the active period, and the listing doesn't describe verification or chat moderation. More in Tablamigo vs TripBFF.

Hostelworld: best if you stay in hostels

Hostelworld is a hostel booking app first. Book a bed and you get city chats for your destination and a chat with people staying at the same hostel, plus events like pub crawls and walking tours. Its paid Social Pass opens city chats and meetups without a booking; our Hostelworld Social Pass comparison has the prices and what it covers.

Good: no extra effort if you're booking hostels anyway. Watch out for: the social side is tied to hostels, so it helps less once you move into a hotel or apartment. For more ways to meet people at hostels, read how to meet people in hostels.

Couchsurfing Hangouts: best for right-now meetups and staying with locals

Couchsurfing is still the place to find a local who'll host you, and hosts don't charge for the stay. Its Hangouts feature shows who's nearby and up for meeting right now, and members post events in their cities.

Good: running since 2003, with references from other members. Watch out for: Couchsurfing's own blog says a paid membership unlocks Hangouts, Events, Communities and stays. See Tablamigo vs Couchsurfing Hangouts.

Timeleft: best for a dinner someone else organizes

Timeleft asks you to take a personality quiz, then books you into a dinner with 4 to 6 people every Wednesday. The venue and a preview of your table are revealed on the day. There are also women-only dinners on Tuesdays at 7pm, runs, Saturday coffee and drinks.

Good: no planning at all, and its listing says it's in 52 countries. Watch out for: it runs on set nights, and you pay for the booking in the app (the US App Store lists purchases from $19.99 to $62.99) as well as for your own food and drinks. More in Tablamigo vs Timeleft. If a fixed night doesn't suit, our guide to eating alone while traveling has other ways to find dinner company.

Meetup: best for meeting locals through interests

Meetup is for recurring groups: language exchanges, running clubs, board games, tech talks. It's the easiest way to meet residents rather than other travelers.

Good: joining groups is free, and it has more than 272,000 App Store ratings. Watch out for: events are often scheduled days ahead, which is hard on a four-day trip.

BFF: best for one-to-one friends on a longer stay

Bumble's friendship app is now called BFF and is run by Geneva Technologies, part of Bumble Inc. You build a profile, chat one to one, or join interest groups. Its listing says every match has passed selfie verification and that everything is free.

Good: photo-verified and free. Watch out for: the listing doesn't mention a travel mode, and chatting before you meet is slow if you're only in town for a few days.

Apps for women

Three apps are for women only: NomadHer (a feed, chat with women travelers nearby, events; no in-app purchases listed on the App Store), Travel Ladies (city group chats, travel buddies, stays with local women; Plus from $4.99 a week) and Tablamigo's women-only plans inside a mixed app. Tourlina is often listed as a women's app, but its listing now says it's open to all genders and includes dating. Details in our solo female travel app guide.

Apps for finding a travel companion for a whole trip

  • GAFFL: post a trip and find someone to share it and split rental car or hotel costs. Paid plans listed from $9.99 on the App Store.
  • Going Solo: nearby travelers, groups and plans, with paid buddy matching ($3.99 one-time, per its listing).
  • Fairytrail: match with people who share a bucket-list activity. Silver membership from $6.99 a week.
  • Travello: now mostly books tours with cashback, with traveler chats alongside.

How to choose

  • In town for a few days? Use a plan-first app (Nomadtable or Tablamigo) and join something for tonight. Chatting first eats the trip.
  • Staying in hostels? Hostelworld's hostel chat costs you nothing extra.
  • Staying a month or more? Add Meetup or BFF to meet people who live there.
  • Nervous about a first meetup? Pick a group plan in a busy place. Read how to meet people while traveling solo for more.

Questions

What is the best app to meet travelers?

For meeting other travelers the same day, Nomadtable has the most App Store ratings of the apps built for it, and Tablamigo is a newer plan-first option for Android and the web. Hostelworld is best if you stay in hostels, and Timeleft if you want a dinner organized for you.

Is there an app for solo travelers to meet each other?

Yes, several. Nomadtable, Tablamigo and TripBFF all show you travelers or plans nearby, Couchsurfing Hangouts shows who's available right now, and Hostelworld connects people in the same hostel or city.

Which is the best travel buddy app?

For company today, a plan-based app like Nomadtable or Tablamigo. For a companion for a whole trip, GAFFL and Going Solo let you post a trip and find someone to share it.

Is there a Tinder for travelers?

If you want dating, use a dating app. The apps on this page are for friendship, and Nomadtable, Tablamigo and Timeleft all say so in their rules or listings. Tourlina is the exception: its listing says it covers travel companions, friendship and dating.

Are apps to meet travelers free?

Most are free to download and use, with paid extras. Tablamigo is the same: {{price_line}} Couchsurfing needs a paid membership for Hangouts and Events, and Timeleft charges per booking. BFF's listing says everything is free.

Which of these apps work in India?

Nomadtable's Android app showed Indian prices on Google Play when we checked, but its iPhone app wasn't on the Indian App Store. Tablamigo works in India on Android and in any browser, and it is free to use, with Plus priced in rupees on the web. More in our comparison of travel buddy apps in India.
