Top Nomadtable Competitors at a Glance

What Nomadtable reviewers mention

Most Nomadtable reviews are positive. The complaints that come up in public reviews are worth knowing whichever app you pick: people using it for dating or hookups, accounts banned without an explanation, busy city chats, bugs and billing problems. These are reviewers' reports, not things we've tested. Nomadtable's developer replies to reviews on the App Store, and its guidelines ban dating-style and spam messaging.

Here's how Tablamigo handles the same problems:

  • Dating and hookups. Plans are real activities, usually in groups, and "Sexual or inappropriate content" is one of the six report reasons.
  • Chats. Group and city chat messages that an AI model flags for harassment, hate or sexual content are blocked before anyone sees them. Reports go to a person.
  • Billing. In the Android app from Google Play, Plus is billed and cancelled in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. On the website and web app, including when you add it to your home screen or desktop, you pay through Razorpay and cancel under Manage subscription. Either way you keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Leaving. Delete your account in the app, or follow our account deletion steps.

Questions

What is the best Nomadtable alternative?

It depends on what you're missing. If you want a free list of travelers nearby, women-only plans, AI moderation of group chats or a web app, try Tablamigo. If you want a different style, TripBFF adds a travel content feed, Couchsurfing Hangouts shows who's available right now, and Timeleft books you a matched dinner. Is Nomadtable a dating app?

No. Nomadtable's community guidelines say it is not a dating app, hookup app or platform for sexual solicitation. Tablamigo has the same rule.

Is Nomadtable free?

Yes, it's free to download and use. Nomadtable Plus costs $8.99 a week, $19.99 a month or $89.99 a year on the US App Store, and a Trip Pass is $11.99 for a week or $24.99 for a month (September 2026).

Its iPhone app wasn't listed on the Indian App Store that day. Tablamigo works in India on Android and in any browser, with Plus priced in rupees on the web.
