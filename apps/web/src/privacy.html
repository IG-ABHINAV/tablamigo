Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Tablamigo is an app that helps solo travelers find and join plans with other travelers nearby. It is operated by Hookstep, a sole proprietorship registered in India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. It covers the Android app, the web app at tablamigo.hookstep.in/app and this website. Questions: support@hookstep.in.

The short version. We collect what is needed to show you plans nearby and let you chat. Other people see only a distance rounded to 0.5 km, not your exact position, unless you choose to share your live location in a chat. {{ads_short}} We don't offer government ID verification and don't collect ID documents. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

3. Who we share data with

{{ads_share}} We use these service providers, only for the purposes listed:

  • Google Cloud — server hosting. Our servers and database are in Singapore.
  • Cloudflare — stores and serves the files you upload (R2), sits in front of our website and API, so it handles the traffic to them, and provides Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits to the website and web app (see the row about visit statistics above).
  • Resend — sends all our emails (sign-in codes, receipts, replies, notifications and the digest). It receives your email address and the email text.
  • Google — Sign in with Google (if you choose it), Google Play billing for purchases in the Android app from Google Play and, if you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging, which delivers notifications to Android phones and sees the notification text. In a browser, notifications go through your browser maker's push service instead. When you aren't signed in to Tablamigo in your browser, the pages of this website (except the legal pages, such as this policy and the terms) and the web app's sign-in screen load Google's sign-in script from accounts.google.com. On the website it loads only after your first scroll, tap, click or key press on a page, never on a timer. If you're signed in to Google in that browser, Google may show a prompt offering to continue to Tablamigo with your Google account. As with any file fetched from Google, loading the script gives Google your IP address and browser details, and to show the prompt Google checks which Google accounts you're signed in to; Google handles this under its own privacy policy. We receive nothing from the prompt unless you pick your account in it, which signs you in, or creates your account, the same way "Continue with Google" does. You can close the prompt or ignore it, and after you close it Google waits a while before showing it again.
  • Razorpay — payments on the website and web app (cards, UPI and other methods Razorpay offers). Razorpay receives your payment details, your email address and an internal account ID; we receive the payment status and IDs.
  • OpenAI — used for two things. (1) Moderation: before they post, we send the text of messages in group, city and trip chats, poll questions and options, comments on moments and plans, moment captions, city tips and buddy-request notes to OpenAI's moderation service, which flags harassment, hate, sexual content, graphic violence and self-harm instructions. We send the text only, not your name or account. Direct messages are not sent. If the check fails or takes longer than 2.5 seconds, the text posts without being checked. (2) Plan ideas: we send the city, the vibe you typed (if any) and your profile interests, never your name, email or exact location.
  • OpenStreetMap-based services (Nominatim, Photon, OpenFreeMap) — our server sends coordinates to Nominatim to look up a city name (your position when your phone reports it or you open a city chat, the meeting point of a plan you post, and the point of a moment you post) and a place name and nearby point to Photon for plan ideas. Your phone contacts Photon directly when you search for a place (the app sends your search text and, for a plan's place search, your position) and OpenFreeMap directly to load the map, so those services see your device's IP address and the map area you look at.

We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety when we believe in good faith that there is a serious risk of harm.

4. International transfers

Our servers are in Singapore and some providers process data in the United States and elsewhere. Where the law requires, transfers rely on the provider's standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

5. How long we keep data

We delete automatically, once a day: unused sign-in codes 1 day after they expire, sign-in sessions 30 days after they expire, in-app notifications 90 days after they are created, usage events 180 days after they are recorded, and live-location shares and expired plan share links 7 days after they end. Everything else stays until you remove it or delete your account, apart from backups and logs, described below.

  • Your account and profile: until you delete your account. We don't delete inactive accounts automatically.
  • Your location: the latest position replaces the one before. A live location you shared keeps its stored point for 7 days after the share ends, then it is deleted; it is not shown to anyone after the share ends.
  • Things you remove in the app: trusted contacts, trips, saved plans, plan comments, plan photos and profile photos are removed from our database when you remove them (the photo files stay in storage, see above). Messages and moments are hidden, not erased: their text, captions and photo addresses stay until you delete your account. City tips, thumbs-ups and ratings can only be removed by deleting your account.
  • Safety records: SOS alerts (with their GPS points) stay until you delete your account. Plan share links are deleted 7 days after they expire.
  • When you delete your account: we remove your profile (including your email address, Instagram handle and location), every file you uploaded, your trips, plan and chat memberships, your moments with their likes and comments, city tips, plan comments, plan photos and ratings, saved plans and private notes, share links, SOS records, trusted and emergency contacts, connections, blocks, notifications, buddy requests, profile views, thumbs-ups, sign-in code, and your sign-in sessions are closed. Your messages are replaced with "[deleted]". Usage events keep their counts but lose your user ID and details until they are deleted at 180 days. Plans you hosted are cancelled. A web (Razorpay) subscription is cancelled for you; a Google Play subscription is not, so cancel it in Google Play first. Deleted photos can stay in Cloudflare's cache for a few hours. The full list is on the deletion page.
  • Kept after deletion: purchase records (Plus subscriptions and Trip Passes you paid for, with the payment records Google or Razorpay returned), reports you made or that were made about you with their support tickets and replies (which hold your email address as the reply address), a log of the service emails we sent you, and a few account details on the anonymised account (when you joined, when you were last active, when your Plus ended, your referral code and who referred you, and your email-notification and display settings). We keep them on the anonymised account for as long as the law requires and no longer than we need them. No automatic deletion date is set yet, and you can ask us to remove them sooner at support@hookstep.in. None of them is shown to other users.
  • Backups: we back up our database every 6 hours. We keep the 10 most recent backups (about two and a half days) on our server and in private off-site storage; older ones are deleted automatically, so data you delete can stay in a backup for up to about 3 days. Backups hold the database only, not uploaded files.
  • Sign-in codes: a code stops working after 10 minutes. We store only a hash of it, and delete it when you sign in, when you delete your account, or automatically a day after it expires.
  • Partnership enquiries and server logs: no automatic deletion period is set. Server logs are not part of your account data, so deleting your account does not change them.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Access, correct, or delete: edit your profile in the app, or delete your account under Profile → Delete account. See how to delete your account.
  • Location and notifications: switch them off at any time in your phone's settings. The app still opens without location, but the map then shows a default area and not where you are.
  • Emails: unsubscribe from optional emails with the link in them. Sign-in codes, receipts and account notices still arrive.
  • Copy of your data or objection: email support@hookstep.in. We reply within 30 days.
  • If you are in the EEA or UK, you can complain to your local data protection authority. In India, you may contact us as the data fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

7. Age limit

Tablamigo is only for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it. See our child safety standards.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Sign-in uses one-time codes, not passwords, and sessions can be revoked by logging out. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
