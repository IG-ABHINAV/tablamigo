Digital nomad meetups: where to find them in any city

Digital nomads find meetups in four places: coworking spaces' community events, Meetup and Facebook groups named "digital nomads [city]", Nomads.com (formerly Nomad List), and apps where you can post a coworking session and have people join.

1. Coworking spaces

The fastest route in a new city. Many coworking spaces run community lunches, workshops, talks or Friday drinks, and some open those to people with a day pass. Ask at the desk what's on this week before you buy a monthly membership.

Watch out for: spaces full of local startups and fixed desks. Friendly, but not where you'll find people to explore with at the weekend.

2. Meetup and Facebook groups

Search Meetup for "digital nomads" plus the city. Popular nomad bases usually have a group with regular coworking days or drinks. On Facebook, "Digital Nomads [city]" and "Expats in [city]" groups post events and answer questions about visas, neighborhoods and SIM cards.

Watch out for: Facebook groups are noisy, with a lot of apartment ads and scams. Use them for events and questions, and don't send deposits to strangers.

3. Nomads.com

Nomads.com, the site formerly called Nomad List, has a meetups page where members organize coworking sessions, dinners and bar meetups in different cities. You need to sign up to take part; check the site for what's included free.

4. Coworking and social plans on Tablamigo

Tablamigo shows plans from travelers and nomads near you on a map, and "work" is one of the plan types. Post "Coworking at a café in Ari, 10am to 2pm, lunch after" and anyone nearby can join and use the plan's group chat to sort out the details. You can also add your next stop and see who'll be in the same city on your dates, and join that city's chat before you land (3 days ahead on a free account, up to a year ahead with Plus).

Watch out for: Tablamigo is new, so in many cities you'll be posting the first plan rather than finding one.

Digital nomad meetups in India

Listed nomad meetups are rare in India, so the coworking and coliving spaces do most of the work. What we found on 11 October 2026:

  • Goa. Goa's backpacker and nomad scene is in the north, around Anjuna, Vagator and Arambol, and the season runs from about November to February. NomadGao runs a coworking and coliving space in Anjuna with long-stay discounts and a community events calendar. Nomads.com's Goa page showed no upcoming meetups and only one ever: Anjuna, on 22 January 2023. Our Goa guide has the hostels and meeting spots.
  • Dharamkot, above McLeod Ganj. NomadGao's second space is here, in the hills. McLeod Ganj's drop-in meditation and cooking classes are the other easy way to meet people staying a while; see our McLeod Ganj guide.
  • Bengaluru. Nomads.com showed no upcoming meetups, and five past ones over the years. The latest, a chai meetup on 10 October 2026, had one attendee listed; the one before was in January 2025.

In all three, a coworking plan on Tablamigo, like "Working from a café in Anjuna, 10 to 2, lunch after", is a way to find out who else is around.

Sources: nomadgao.com and nomads.com's meetup pages for India, Goa and Bengaluru, checked 11 October 2026.

Making nomad friendships stick

  • Go to the same thing twice. A weekly coworking day or language exchange turns faces into friends by the second or third week.
  • Host something small. A Sunday brunch or a hike for five is easier to fill, and to enjoy, than a big event.
  • Line up your next city early. Add the trip on Tablamigo and you'll see who overlaps with you there, so you can plan a first-night dinner before you arrive.
  • Mix in locals. Classes, sports clubs and language exchanges get you out of the nomad bubble.

Questions

How do digital nomads meet each other?

Mostly through coworking spaces' community events, Meetup and Facebook groups for their city, Nomads.com meetups and apps where they can post or join plans, like Tablamigo. Many also meet through shared housing and classes.

What are some good groups for digital nomads to join?

Look for a Meetup group called "Digital Nomads" plus your city, the "Digital Nomads [city]" group on Facebook, and the community at the coworking space you use. Nomads.com also runs a members' community with meetups.

Is there an app for digital nomad meetups?

Yes. Tablamigo lets you post a coworking session, dinner or day trip and have nomads nearby join, with a group chat for each plan. Meetup and Nomads.com also list nomad events.

Which cities have the most digital nomad meetups?

Nomad hubs like Lisbon, Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Bali, Mexico City, Medellín and Da Nang tend to have regular events because so many remote workers pass through. Our city guides list where people meet in each.

Are there digital nomad meetups in India?

Very few are listed. On 11 October 2026, Nomads.com showed no upcoming meetups anywhere in India and six past ones, five of them in Bengaluru; Goa's only one was in Anjuna in January 2023. Coworking and coliving spaces such as NomadGao in Anjuna and Dharamkot run their own community events, and on Tablamigo you can post a coworking plan for remote workers nearby to join.
