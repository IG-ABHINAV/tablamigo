Travel buddy and partner apps in India: 9 compared

No single travel buddy app is best for India. Some find you one person for a whole trip, some find you people to do things with today, and two of the best known, Nomadtable and TripBFF, have no iPhone app in India at all. Here are nine travel buddy and travel partner apps, checked against their Indian store listings.

We make Tablamigo, and it's on this list, marked as ours, so weigh that. Facts about the other apps come from their Google Play listings in India, their Indian App Store listings and their own websites, checked on October 6, 2026, except SyncTrip, Couchsurfing's prices and the apps under "Other apps", checked on October 11, 2026. Prices are what Google Play India lists for in-app purchases, in rupees, unless a price is marked otherwise. The order groups apps by what they do. It isn't a ranking.

The quick answer

Is Nomadtable available in India? On Android, yes. On iPhone, no: its app isn't on the Indian App Store (rechecked October 11, 2026). The same goes for TripBFF.

  • One person to travel with for a trek or a whole trip: Travel Buddy (swipe-style matching, with booking) or GAFFL (you post a trip and others connect). Both work on Android and iPhone in India.
  • A group trip someone else posts: SyncTrip, built in India, where one person posts a trip, to places like Manali or Spiti, and others join.
  • People to do things with today: a plan-based app. Tablamigo (ours) and Solomigo show plans on a map. Nomadtable and TripBFF also help you meet travelers nearby, but neither has an iPhone app in India.
  • A bed with a built-in crowd: Hostelworld if you book hostels, Couchsurfing if you want a local host (Hangouts needs a paid membership).

1. Travel Buddy: best for matching with a travel partner

Travel Buddy ("Travel Buddy: AI Buddy Finder" on the stores) matches you with other travelers. You swipe through profiles, filter by location, age, gender and interests, and chat one to one or in groups. It also has an AI trip planner, a community feed and in-app booking for flights, hotels and experiences. Its own website calls it "India's fastest growing Travelers Community".

Good: it's on both stores in India. On Google Play it has 1M+ installs and 4.6 stars from 7.76K reviews; on the Indian App Store it's 4.2 from 88 ratings. The listing describes a selfie check for profiles. Watch out for: the Android app contains ads, and matching works best when other people are going where you are. A premium membership adds things like zero convenience fees on bookings and unlimited AI chat, per its listing.

Source: Travel Buddy's Google Play India and Indian App Store listings and beatravelbuddy.com, checked October 6, 2026.

2. GAFFL: best for finding someone to share a whole trip

GAFFL is built around trips. You post where and when you're going, travelers with a similar trip tap Connect, and you plan in chat. Its main pitch is splitting costs, such as a rental car or a hotel room. Its India page shows member profiles from cities across India, including Mumbai, Bangalore and New Delhi; the selection changes each time the page loads.

Good: Android and iPhone, rated 4.8 on Google Play from 1.5K reviews and 4.6 on the Indian App Store from 131 ratings. Its India page says verification includes social media, phone and government ID checks. Watch out for: its listing also mentions a nearby, recently-active travelers list for spontaneous meetups; we haven't tested how busy that is in Indian cities. Its core flow is posting a trip, so matches depend on someone going where you are. The paid GAFFL Unlimited plan is sold in the app (Google Play India lists in-app purchases of ₹1,050 to ₹6,700 per item).

Source: GAFFL's Google Play India and Indian App Store listings and gogaffl.com/find-a-travel-buddy/india, checked October 6, 2026.

3. SyncTrip: best for joining group trips posted in India

SyncTrip is built in India by SyncTrip Digital Private Limited. One person posts a trip, to places such as Manali, Goa, Ladakh, Rishikesh or Spiti, and others join it and plan in a group chat. Its Google Play listing now describes it more widely, as an app for making friends through plans nearby: board-game nights, sports, café meetups, bike rides and weekend group trips. Its App Store listing says the newest of those plans are in Gurgaon, Delhi NCR and Chandigarh.

Good: it's on both stores in India, with 4.8 stars from 70 reviews on Google Play (5K+ installs, updated October 10, 2026) and 5.0 from 25 ratings on the Indian App Store. Its App Store listing says profiles are "100% ID-verified" but not how, and the Google Play listing says the team reviews every report. Watch out for: it's small, so check whether trips are posted for your dates before relying on it. Google Play lists no in-app purchases; the Indian App Store lists a ₹49 SyncTrip Trial and ₹199 SyncTrip Plus. For how group trips compare with other ways to travel with strangers, see solo trips with strangers in India.

Source: SyncTrip's Google Play India and Indian App Store listings, checked October 11, 2026.

4. Tablamigo (ours): best for joining plans on a map, free to use

Tablamigo shows plans from travelers nearby on a map, listed by start time with plans from Plus members first. Plans might be a Goa beach day, chai and a walk in Old Delhi, or a trek near Rishikesh. Tap join and you're in that plan's group chat. You can post your own plan, see a free list of travelers nearby with an age filter, add a trip to see who'll be in the same city on your dates, and join city chats.

Good: {{price_line}} Women can post women-only plans that only other women can see and join. There's a selfie check, 18+ only, AI moderation of group and city chats, and distances rounded to 0.5 km. Watch out for: it's new, so in most places you'll be posting the first plan, and it has no ratings yet. There's no iPhone app: on iPhone it's a web app in Safari, and notifications need you to add it to your Home Screen.

Source: our own app and our Google Play listing, checked October 6, 2026.

5. Solomigo: best for plans on a map if you're on iPhone

Solomigo is a plan-first app with the same basic idea as ours: a live map of plans (coffee, treks, road trips, a cab to split), where you ask to join, the host says yes and the plan gets a group chat. Its site says people review new profiles and plans before they appear, every member does a selfie check and hosts can make a plan women-only. Its FAQ says community density is strongest in Indian metros at launch, and its terms are governed by Indian law, with Bengaluru courts.

Good: it has an app on both Android and iPhone, which we don't. Discovering, joining and hosting are free, with daily limits, per its FAQ. Watch out for: it's brand new. The Android listing shows 10+ installs and the App Store listing, released August 17, 2026, has no ratings yet. The same caveat applies to ours: check whether anyone is posting in your city. An optional membership removes the limits and hosts can boost a plan (Google Play India lists in-app purchases of ₹290 to ₹10,000 per item).

Source: Solomigo's Google Play India and Indian App Store listings and solomigo.com, checked October 6, 2026.

6. Nomadtable: best for joining activities, on Android

Nomadtable shows activities other travelers post nearby, such as dinner, sightseeing or a night out, and you join one. You can also add your next destination and get put in a group chat with travelers there on the same dates.

Good: 1M+ installs on Google Play and 4.2 stars from 102K reviews. Watch out for: in India it's Android only. Its iPhone app loads on the US App Store but not the Indian one, so with an Indian App Store account you can't install it. Google Play India lists in-app purchases of ₹750 to ₹7,400 per item. 7. TripBFF: best for travelers nearby and on your next trip, on Android

TripBFF's listing says you can find nearby travelers, join local meetups and see who's going on the same upcoming trips, so you can connect before you arrive.

Good: 100K+ installs on Google Play, updated October 2, 2026. Watch out for: like Nomadtable, it's Android only in India: its App Store page loads in the US but not in India. Google Play India lists in-app purchases of ₹520 to ₹6,400 per item. More in Tablamigo vs TripBFF.

Source: TripBFF's Google Play India listing and its US and Indian App Store pages, checked October 6, 2026.

8. Hostelworld: best if you stay in hostels

Hostelworld is a hostel booking app first, and it lists hostels across India, including Goa. Book a bed and you can join chats for your destination and your hostel. Its paid Social Pass opens chats and events without a booking.

Good: 5M+ installs on Google Play (4.6 from 81K reviews) and 4.8 on the Indian App Store from 7,756 ratings, with no extra effort if you're booking hostels anyway. Watch out for: the social side is tied to hostels. Social Pass is priced in euros on its site (€4.99 for a week, €9.99 a month, €19.99 for three months, €59.99 a year, each a one-time purchase), and the page doesn't say which cities it covers, so check inside the app before paying for an Indian town.

Source: Hostelworld's Google Play India and Indian App Store listings, hostelworld.com/socialpass and its India hostels page, checked October 6, 2026.

9. Couchsurfing: best for staying with local hosts

Couchsurfing is where you find a local who'll host you, and hosts don't charge for the stay. Its Hangouts feature shows members nearby who want to meet right now.

Good: 5M+ installs on Google Play (4.0 from 121K reviews) and 4.6 on the Indian App Store from 2,706 ratings. Watch out for: its blog says an active membership is required to use Hangouts, and Google Play India listed in-app purchases of ₹290 to ₹3,400 per item on October 11, 2026. We couldn't check how many hosts or Hangouts are active in a given Indian town. More in Tablamigo vs Couchsurfing Hangouts.

Source: Couchsurfing's Google Play India and Indian App Store listings and the Couchsurfing blog (Hangouts post updated August 11, 2026), checked October 6, 2026; in-app prices rechecked October 11, 2026.

How to choose a travel companion app in India

  • Want company tonight? Use a plan-based app: Tablamigo, Solomigo, Nomadtable or TripBFF. Check which of them has plans in your city before you commit to one.
  • Want a partner for a trek or a week? Post your route and dates in Travel Buddy or GAFFL early. Matches depend on someone else going the same way.
  • Want a group? SyncTrip for trips other users post, or a group trip with an operator. Solo trips with strangers in India compares operator trips, hostel-run trips and going alone, with a checklist for vetting an operator.
  • Looking for a travel partner for Goa or Manali? Our Goa and Manali guides list the hostels and meeting spots where people heading the same way stay, checked on their own sites.
  • On iPhone? Solomigo, Travel Buddy, GAFFL, SyncTrip, Hostelworld and Couchsurfing have Indian App Store listings. Nomadtable and TripBFF don't. Tablamigo runs in Safari.
  • Traveling as a woman? Tablamigo and Solomigo both offer women-only plans, and NomadHer is for women only. On Tablamigo, gender is what each person picks on their profile, so read what women-only plans do and don't guarantee, and solo female travel in India for hostels and helplines.

Whichever you use, meet somewhere busy first, tell someone where you're going, and never send money to someone you met in an app. A selfie badge only shows that a selfie matched the profile photo. Emergency numbers and local safety tips are in our solo travel in India guide.

Is Nomadtable available in India?

What is the best travel buddy app in India?

For people to do things with today, try a plan-based app: Tablamigo (ours) and Solomigo show plans on a map, while Nomadtable and TripBFF are Android only in India.

Is it safe to travel with someone you met on an app?

Treat a first meeting as a short, public one. Pick a group plan or a café, tell someone where you're going, keep the chat in the app until you've met, and don't share a room or a booking until you know each other. Look for verification where the app has it, and report anything that feels off.
